How the rows are selected

  • Trending (TMDB): weekly TMDB trends, filtered to titles available on the provider in India.
  • Popular (TMDB): TMDB popularity among verified titles on the provider.
  • New: recent movie releases or series premieres currently available on the provider.

New means the title's release date. It does not mean newly added to a platform. These are TMDB rankings; official platform charts and originals collections are not available in this version.

Availability includes subscription, free and ad-supported offers. Separate reseller channels and rental/purchase offers are excluded. Availability can change or be incomplete.
